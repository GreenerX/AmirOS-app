# AmirOS brand mark

The approved identity is the **Quiet Monogram**: a softly rounded `A`/`M`
construction with a small conversational detail in its center.

## Assets

- `amiros-mark.svg` — standalone mark for navigation and compact UI.
- `amiros-app-icon.svg` — emerald rounded-square app icon.
- `amiros-wordmark.svg` — horizontal AmirOS mark and wordmark lockup.

## Core colors

- Emerald: `#078A56`
- Charcoal: `#17202B`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Keep the mark flat. Do not add gradients, shadows, outlines, rotations, or
WhatsApp-like phone imagery. Preserve clear space of at least one-quarter of
the mark's width on every side.
