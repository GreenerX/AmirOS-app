07

Your relationships are personal.
AmirOS treats them that way.

You're in control

Choose the relationships that get help.

Choose which conversations AmirOS remembers and whether help is Off, Suggest or Auto for each one.

Review before action

You stay involved.

Suggested replies can be edited before sending. Remembered context and plans can be reviewed, confirmed or dismissed. Auto replies only run for chats you choose.

Local control center

Built around your Mac.

Your saved AmirOS data and control center run locally on your Mac. When AmirOS answers a question or prepares a summary or reply, the relevant message context is sent to OpenAI using the account you connect.

WhatsApp connection Connect AmirOS by scanning a QR code, like linking another device. Your Mac needs to stay running while AmirOS is active.
